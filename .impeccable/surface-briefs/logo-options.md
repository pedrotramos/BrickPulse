# BrickPulse logo exploration

Mode: Experience. A standalone comparison artifact, not a redesign of the application.

The user rejected the initial four-tile app icon and requested exactly six new
logos. They subsequently approved original option 3 (Frequência, #B94B2C), after
rejecting a requested fusion of 3 and 4. Apply the exact original option 3 to
the app and favicon; preserve the six vectors as exploration history. The
direction contract below records the original comparison artifact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Six authored, materially different vector symbols, compared at equal
scale and with the same BrickPulse lettering, so shape rather than presentation
bias carries the choice. These are candidate identities, not a selected world.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the app's light analytical presentation and self-hosted
Instrument Sans. Candidates may explore distinct accent colors; the gallery
provides uniform monochrome and reversed views to separate shape from color.

STORY: See all six at once, compare each at icon size and reversed, optionally
download its SVG, then tell the assistant the preferred number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A short introduction and a three-column, two-row comparison
board on desktop; a single column on mobile. Each numbered option has a large
horizontal lockup, a description, a reversed sample and 16/24/32px symbols.
The signature interaction switches all six between color, monochrome and dark
background together, without moving or reordering them.

FORM: Narrow, code-led vector exploration requested by the user. No direction
tournament, raster image generation, app release, or chosen-identity migration.
Numbers are required selection references, not decorative section numbering.

FINISH: Inspect desktop/mobile together; deliver a PNG comparison plus six
downloadable SVG families. Independent review covers this artifact only.
