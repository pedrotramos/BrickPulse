# BrickPulse — Foco + Frequência

Mode: Experience. Rejected exploration, retained as design history.

The user asked to combine original options 3 (five-bar frequency symbol) and 4
(geometric eye), preferring option 3's exact orange, #B94B2C. They then rejected
the fusion and chose original option 3. Do not apply this fusion to the app.
The direction contract below records the discarded study.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Replace the eye's central diamond with a five-bar pulse, making one
coherent emblem rather than placing two logos side by side.

OWN-WORLD: Original eye silhouette, an enlarged inner aperture to give the
waveform breathing room, rounded bars, exact orange #B94B2C, inherited
graphite lettering and Instrument Sans. No gradients or decorative depth.

STORY: Recognize the eye from option 4, see the frequency from option 3 at its
center, and assess the result in the requested color before approving it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A large horizontal signature on white, followed by the isolated
symbol, a reversed signature and actual 16/24/32px samples. Mobile stacks the
same proofs. Download links expose all six SVG variants directly.

FORM: User-pinned refinement of existing vector candidates, code-led. No new
visual-world tournament, unrelated alternatives or app deployment. At 16px the
eye silhouette leads; the internal waveform reads best from 24px.

FINISH: Batched desktop/mobile evidence, SVG validation and independent review
of this candidate sheet only. Preserve the production identity and global
design documentation until the user approves the fusion.
