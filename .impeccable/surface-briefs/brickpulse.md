# BrickPulse application

Mode: Operate. Audience: platform engineers with an executive overview. Scope: reconstructed application and all existing analytical routes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Financial observability organized around evidence and actions, with one consistent view from account totals to resource detail.

OWN-WORLD: Light cool-neutral canvas, white work surfaces, graphite type and petrol selection/action accent. Self-hosted Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono typography (locally available substitution for IBM Plex, disclosed during implementation). Tabular numbers, precise tables, semantic status colors and the user-approved original option 3 logo: five rounded frequency bars in orange #B94B2C. The orange belongs to the logo; interface actions remain petrol.

STORY: Read the current spend and estimated opportunity, identify the best action, inspect its evidence and follow the concrete instructions in Databricks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 232px sidebar, workspace/language toolbar, clear page title and freshness, three contextual financial measures, a wide daily-spend chart and a compact opportunity breakdown. Prioritized resource rows continue below. Signature interaction: account/workspace filtering maintains context across every route; selecting a resource reveals evidence and options in a focused detail view. Motion only communicates these state changes, with reduced-motion support.

FORM: Analytical workbench, explicitly approved in the implementation plan; the approved direction overrides concept alternatives. Code-led implementation, no image assets required.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
